A Note from the Atelier
"Iyawoooo, Oko Iyawoooo! Sé dáadáa lè wà?"

My name is Fiponmileoluwa — most people call me Fifii. I'm the creative director of Abánitúnrase. Lawyer by training, stylist by calling. Mostly stylist — I've been pulling looks under the radar since university, and the moment I was called to the Nigerian Bar I knew exactly what I wanted to do with the rest of my life.

We built this house with one passion: that our brides, grooms, and clients arrive in looks that stand out, stay clean, and remain timeless. Òkó iyàwó, iyàwó — I look forward to working with you.
